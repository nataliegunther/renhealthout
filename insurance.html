Insurance & FAQ

Making care accessible.

We work with most major insurance plans and offer private pay and sliding-scale options, so cost doesn't have to stand between you and the care you need.

Insurance we accept

  • BlueCross BlueShield
  • Midlands Choice
  • UnitedHealthcare (UHC & UBH)
  • Molina Healthcare
  • Optum
  • Medica
  • Aetna
  • Cigna
  • Evernorth
Don't see your plan? Please call our front desk at 402-249-0377. We may still be able to work with your insurance, and we also see out-of-network patients.
Payment

Clear and fair

Patients are responsible for deductibles, co-pays, co-insurance, and any services their plan doesn't cover.

  • Most major insurance plans accepted
  • Out-of-network patients welcome
  • Private pay options
  • Sliding-scale fees available. Just ask.
Frequently asked questions

Good questions

How do I get started?
Give us a call at 402-249-0377. A team member will answer your questions and help you schedule your first visit.
How do I schedule counseling?
For counseling and psychotherapy, call 402-937-1920.
How do I sign in to the patient portal?
Click Patient Portal at the top of any page. If you haven't set up your portal account yet, call our office and we'll send you an invitation.
What if I can't afford care?
Please talk to us. We offer private pay and sliding-scale options and will work with you to find something that fits.
Is my visit confidential?
Yes. Your privacy matters to us, including for sensitive services like Hepatitis C, HIV, PrEP, and mental health care.
What are your hours?
Monday through Thursday, 9am–5pm, and Friday, 9am–12pm.
