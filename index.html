How we can help

Care for the whole person

From everyday health needs to mental health and specialized prevention, our care is built around you.

Primary & Family Care

Checkups, chronic illness management, and acute care for established patients, for the whole family.

Behavioral Medicine

Thoughtful medication management for depression, anxiety, ADHD, bipolar disorder, and substance use.

Integrative Medicine

Functional medicine that looks for the root cause, including hormone support through life's transitions.

Hepatitis C & HIV Prevention

Confidential screening, education and treatment, including PrEP and PEP for STI prevention.

Counseling & Psychotherapy

Licensed therapists for mental health and addiction, working hand-in-hand with your medical care.

Wellness & Weight

Support for weight loss, stress, women's health, and building habits that last.
