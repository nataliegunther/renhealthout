About us

Care that sees the whole you.

Renewed Health Outreach was built on a simple belief: everyone deserves healthcare where they feel welcome, safe, and respected, and where someone takes the time to find out what's really going on.

Meet your provider

Jodi Fuehrer

APRN, NP-C · Nurse Practitioner

Jodi has practiced as a nurse practitioner for more than 18 years and has worked in medicine for over 35. Her care is integrative: she looks at the connection between mind, body, and spirit, and encourages every patient to take an active role in their own health.

Above all, Jodi has worked to create a place free of stigma, where patients can talk openly and know they'll be treated with dignity, whatever brings them in.
