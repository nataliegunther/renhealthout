Our services

Medical. Behavioral medicine. Wellness.

We care for your physical and mental health together, because they were never really separate. Here's how we can help.

Primary & Family Care

Ongoing care for you and your family, with a provider who knows your history.

  • Wellness visits and checkups
  • Chronic illness management
  • Acute care for established patients
  • Women's health

Behavioral Medicine

A non-stigmatizing approach to mental health, with careful medication management.

  • Depression & anxiety
  • ADHD
  • Bipolar disorder & mood disorders
  • Substance use disorder & dual diagnosis
  • Trauma, PTSD & OCD

Hepatitis C & HIV Outreach

Confidential, judgment-free prevention and treatment, one of our top specialties.

  • Hepatitis C screening, education & treatment
  • HIV screening & education
  • PrEP (pre-exposure prevention)
  • PEP (post-exposure prevention)
  • STI prevention

Functional & Integrative Medicine

We look for the underlying cause of illness, not just the symptoms on the surface.

  • Root-cause evaluation
  • Hormone regulation through life's transitions
  • Weight loss support
  • Stress & lifestyle care

Counseling & Psychotherapy

Licensed therapists for individuals facing mental health and addiction challenges.

  • Addiction & codependency
  • Self-esteem & stress
  • Trauma & TBI
  • Scheduling: 402-937-1920

Testing & Evaluation

Thorough assessment so your care plan is built on a clear understanding of what's happening.

  • Mental health evaluation
  • ADHD testing
  • Lab work & screenings
